⭐ Los 3 Prompts de Animación (Meta AI)

1. SLIDER LATERAL Movimiento

Crea paralaje y sensación de profundidad de lado a lado. Ideal para bosques, filas de flores y composiciones horizontales.

slow lateral camera slider movement from left to right. Soft 3D parallax effect separating the foreground elements from the background. Elegant depth of field, organic and cinematic camera motion.
2. PUSH-IN 3D Volumen

Avanza hacia el centro y da relieve tridimensional real a las texturas. Ideal para galaxias, centros de luz y detalles de pintura.

A slow, steady cinematic camera push-in towards the center of the painting. The textures, paint strokes, and objects on the canvas extrude and gain real 3D volume, transforming into tangible physical forms. Subtle 3D parallax effect, gentle depth of field shift, smooth and organic motion, 4k.
3. ESTÁTICO (Elemento Vivo) Enfoque

Congela toda la escena excepto un elemento específico. Ideal para velas, lluvia, olas, auroras y hojas que caen.

Static camera shot. Elegant depth of field and cinematic lighting. The entire scene remains completely still, except for a single random element that has subtle, organic and gentle motion. Soft 3D parallax layers separating foreground from background.
