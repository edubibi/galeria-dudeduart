Beyond the Visual

The Sensory Art Collection

A private exhibition showcasing a revolutionary approach to tactile aesthetics. Created by Eduardo Ramirez, a veteran music producer turned texture pioneer, this collection features 48 unique structural styles including the signature Gessolux and Espatac-Extreme.

Designed exclusively for high-end interior spaces, these large-format abstracts break the boundary between the visual and the physical, commanding presence through deep, aggressive textures and sober elegance.
